Diablo IV on Apple Silicon

Diablo IV has no Mac version. It runs through the Windows Battle.net client that Soju sets up: on an M4 Pro with macOS 26.5 the free trial started, played its opening cinematic, let us create a character and loaded into the first area, drawn with Apple's D3DMetal.

The quick version

brew install BCD1210/soju/soju
soju install          # choose Battle.net
soju battlenet        # sign in, open Diablo IV, press Try For Free or Install, then Play

Or download the Soju app, open Platforms and choose Battle.net.

What you need

ItemDetail
MacApple Silicon. Checked on an M4 Pro with macOS 26.5; other Macs are untested.
AccountA Battle.net account. The free trial needs no purchase; Blizzard sets its level cap and how long it is offered.
Apple's Game Porting ToolkitSoju downloads it during setup. soju doctor says if anything is missing.
Disk97.4 GB without High-Resolution Assets, 181 GB with them, as Battle.net reported it. Untick High-Resolution Assets on the install screen to save the space.

Installing

  1. In Battle.net, pick Diablo IV in the game row and press Try For Free (or Install if you own it).
  2. On the second install screen, untick High-Resolution Assets unless you have the space.
  3. Play turns on before the download ends. Here it did at about 73 percent, and the game started while the rest downloaded.

The game opens full screen. The first screens ask about the screen reader, brightness and accessibility options, then the opening cinematic plays for several minutes.

What we checked

  • Battle.net installed the trial and started the game.
  • The setup screens, the opening cinematic with subtitles, class selection and character creation all drew correctly.
  • The game loaded into the first area (Icehowl Taiga) with the interface, minimap and quest shown, and the character moved.

What we did not check

  • Sound, frame rate, combat and long sessions.
  • Playing with other people, and the full game beyond the trial.
  • Other Macs and macOS versions.

Played further on your Mac? Turn on Show FPS in the game's sheet in Soju and tell us the number in #compatibility on Discord or in a compatibility report; it goes on the Diablo IV page.

Questions

Does Diablo IV run on Apple Silicon Macs?

Through the Windows Battle.net client that Soju installs, Diablo IV started on an M4 Pro with macOS 26.5: the opening cinematic played, a character was created and the game loaded into the first area, where the character moved. It is drawn with Apple's D3DMetal. Sound, frame rate and long sessions were not checked.

Can I try Diablo IV on a Mac without buying it?

Yes, while Blizzard offers its free trial. In Battle.net, open Diablo IV and press Try For Free. The trial stops at a level cap that Blizzard sets.

How much disk space does Diablo IV need on a Mac?

Battle.net asked for 181 GB with High-Resolution Assets and 97.4 GB without them. The Play button turned on at about 73 percent of the download.
