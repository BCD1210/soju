Which one fits

  • Your games are on Battle.net, or need the Windows Steam client, and you want a free tool: Soju.
  • You want a commercial product with support and the widest range of Windows software: CrossOver.
  • Your library is on Epic, GOG or Amazon and you want one launcher for it: Heroic. Soju also installs the Epic and GOG clients.
  • You want to turn one Windows program into its own Mac app: Sikarugir.
  • You already use Whisky: the community fork keeps the app you know.

What Soju has run

All on an M4 Pro with macOS 26.5.

  • Diablo II: Resurrected, Battle.net: plays online. Needs macOS 26.4 or later. Guide
  • StarCraft: Remastered, Battle.net: a single-player match starts. Multiplayer not tried. Guide
  • Hogwarts Legacy, Epic: installs and plays with D3DMetal.
  • Guilt Free, Steam: plays with the DXMT setup. Guide
  • Retro Classics, Xbox PC: reaches the main menu.
  • GOG GALAXY: login and library work; few games tried.

What Soju does not do

  • Games with kernel anti-cheat (EAC, BattlEye, Vanguard) do not run.
  • The list of checked games is short. A launcher opening does not mean every game in it works.
  • Xbox PC support is experimental.
  • It needs an Apple Silicon Mac. Steam games need macOS 26 or later.

Questions

What replaced Whisky on Mac?

Nothing took its place one for one. A community fork keeps the Whisky app going, CrossOver is the commercial option its developer pointed people to, and Soju is a free app that installs the Windows Battle.net, Steam, Epic and GOG launchers. Which fits depends on where your games are.

Is Soju better than CrossOver?

They do different jobs. CrossOver covers far more Windows software and comes with support; it costs $74 with a year of updates. Soju is free and open source, and covers the launchers and games it has checked: Battle.net, the Windows Steam client, Epic and GOG. Soju's engine is built from the sources CodeWeavers publishes.

Does Heroic run Battle.net or Steam games?

Heroic is a launcher for Epic, GOG and Amazon libraries. Battle.net and Steam are not among its stores. For those two, the options here are Soju, CrossOver, or a wrapper you set up yourself.

Which of these are free?

Soju, the Whisky fork, Heroic, Sikarugir and Mythic are free. CrossOver is paid and has a free trial.
