Sniper Ghost Warrior Contracts on Mac

Mac players report it runs

A Mac player reported it running through Highball (M5 2026-09-15); not verified by Highball's maintainer. Not run in Soju yet; Soju's engines differ, so results can too. Their notes: Launches without its textures and assets for the one report so far, on an early Wine 11 engine (r8). Not diagnosed, and not tried on a current engine.

Linux (Proton) reference: 10 of 10 player reports from the last two years say it runs (ProtonDB, as of 2026-11-02, ODbL).

How to try it with Soju

brew install BCD1210/soju/soju
soju install          # choose Steam (macOS 26 or later)
soju steam         # sign in, install the game, press Play

Or download the Soju app and add Steam under Platforms. Needs an Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or newer) and macOS 14 or later.

Tried it? Tell us in Discord or send a compatibility report and this page gets updated.
