Heavy Rain on Mac

Mac players report problems

A Mac player reported problems through Highball (1/5) (M1 2026-10-29). Not run in Soju yet; Soju's engines differ, so results can too. Their notes: Crashes a few seconds into its first loading screen on an M1 (D3DMetal's DirectX 11 at 59.5 fps on that screen, then exit). Beyond: Two Souls, from the same studio's PC ports, runs on DXMT, which is the next mode to try.

Linux (Proton) reference: 33 of 39 player reports from the last two years say it runs (ProtonDB, as of 2026-11-02, ODbL).

Mac data: Highball database (CC0).

How to try it with Soju

brew install BCD1210/soju/soju
soju install          # choose Steam (macOS 26 or later)
soju steam         # sign in, install the game, press Play

Or download the Soju app and add Steam under Platforms. Needs an Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or newer) and macOS 14 or later.

Tried it? Tell us in #compatibility on Discord or send a compatibility report and this page gets updated.
