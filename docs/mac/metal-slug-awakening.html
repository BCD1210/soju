Metal Slug: Awakening on Mac

Does not run: anti-cheat

Highball's database lists it as blocked (anti-cheat or publisher). Not run in Soju yet; Soju's engines differ, so results can too. Their notes: Does not start. Its launcher runs, then the game stops on a breakpoint, which is how Tencent's Anti-Cheat Expert refuses to run. It fails the same way on Linux.

Linux (Proton) reference: 4 of 4 player reports from the last two years say it runs (ProtonDB, as of 2026-11-02, ODbL).

Mac data: Highball database (CC0).
