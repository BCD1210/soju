WRATH: Aeon of Ruin on Mac

Mac players report it runs

A Mac player reported it running through Highball (5/5) (M5 Pro 2026-10-19); not verified by Highball's maintainer. Not run in Soju yet; Soju's engines differ, so results can too. Their notes: Reported perfect on DXVK with the default engine, close to 120 fps on an M5 Pro, with short hitches the first time new effects load. A native build of the game's engine for Apple silicon and Intel Macs exists at Mac Source Ports (https://www.macsourceports.com/game/wrath). It runs with the game files from a Steam or GOG copy, without achievements (Swiss-Mac-User, highball-db#571).

Linux (Proton) reference: 4 of 4 player reports from the last two years say it runs (ProtonDB, as of 2026-11-02, ODbL).

Mac data: Highball database (CC0).

How to try it with Soju

brew install BCD1210/soju/soju
soju install          # choose Steam (macOS 26 or later)
soju steam         # sign in, install the game, press Play

Or download the Soju app and add Steam under Platforms. Needs an Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or newer) and macOS 14 or later.

Tried it? Tell us in Discord or send a compatibility report and this page gets updated.
