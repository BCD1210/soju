Left 4 Dead 2 on Mac

Mac players report problems

A Mac player reported problems through Highball (2/5) (M2 2026-09-16). Not run in Soju yet; Soju's engines differ, so results can too. Their notes: Runs well on an M2 Air once workshop add-ons are not loaded automatically; with them the campaign froze at load on DXVK and crawled on Wine's Direct3D. The mode of the clean run is not recorded yet. A Source game, so DXVK is the expected mode. On an M1 with the Wine 11 engine and DXMT it played 49 minutes without trouble, with small dips when big hordes come.

Linux (Proton) reference: 229 of 263 player reports from the last two years say it runs (ProtonDB, as of 2026-11-02, ODbL).

Mac data: Highball database (CC0).

How to try it with Soju

brew install BCD1210/soju/soju
soju install          # choose Steam (macOS 26 or later)
soju steam         # sign in, install the game, press Play

Or download the Soju app and add Steam under Platforms. Needs an Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or newer) and macOS 14 or later.

Tried it? Tell us in #compatibility on Discord or send a compatibility report and this page gets updated.
