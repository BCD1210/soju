NARUTO SHIPPUDEN: Ultimate Ninja STORM 4 on Mac

Runs on Mac (Highball test)

Highball's maintainer ran it on an Apple Silicon Mac with Highball's own Wine engine and d3dmetal. Not run in Soju yet; Soju's engines differ, so results can too. Their notes: Plays on D3DMetal. Tested 2026-09-10 on an M1 Pro, macOS 26.6.2, engine x64-sikarugir10.0_6-r2: a fight renders and plays correctly at 1920x1080 on D3DMetal, which is what this row now tells Highball to pick. Linux (Proton) reference: 4 of 4 player reports from the last two years say it runs (ProtonDB, as of 2026-11-02, ODbL).

Mac data: Highball database (CC0).

How to try it with Soju

brew install BCD1210/soju/soju
soju install          # choose Steam (macOS 26 or later)
soju steam         # sign in, install the game, press Play

Or download the Soju app and add Steam under Platforms. Needs an Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or newer) and macOS 14 or later.

Tried it? Tell us in #compatibility on Discord or send a compatibility report and this page gets updated.
