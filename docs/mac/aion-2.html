Soju is a free, open-source app that installs the official Windows game launchers on Apple Silicon Macs.

AION 2 on Mac

Does not run: anti-cheat

Highball's database lists it as blocked (anti-cheat or publisher). Not run in Soju yet; Soju's engines differ, so results can too. Their notes: Does not start, NCGuard, the game's anti-cheat, stops it. With msync NCGuard exits about 8 seconds in with 0xE0000015 on a fresh environment (2 of 2 tries). With sync off NCGuard finishes its setup, then the game spins on its splash screen at about 157% CPU for over seven minutes without loading anything (3 of 3). With esync Steam crashes before the game starts (1 of 1).

Linux (Proton) reference: 78 of 92 player reports from the last two years say it runs (ProtonDB, as of 2026-11-02, ODbL).

Mac data: Highball database (CC0).
