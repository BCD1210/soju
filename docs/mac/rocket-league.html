Does not run: anti-cheat

Highball's database lists it as blocked (anti-cheat or publisher). Not run in Soju yet; Soju's engines differ, so results can too. Their notes: Since 2026-04-28 (Season 22) Rocket League on PC needs Easy Anti-Cheat enabled to queue for online matches, private matches and tournaments, on both the Epic Games Store and Steam (for accounts that still own the delisted Steam version).
