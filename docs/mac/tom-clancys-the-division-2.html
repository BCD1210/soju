Tom Clancy’s The Division 2 on Mac

Does not run: anti-cheat

Highball's database lists it as blocked (anti-cheat or publisher). Not run in Soju yet; Soju's engines differ, so results can too. Their notes: Not playable on a Mac. The game uses Easy Anti-Cheat, which works on Linux through its runtime for Proton, and that runtime has no macOS build. The reporter confirms the anti-cheat check crashes the game window. They could only keep playing by repeatedly stopping the check from running and do not recommend it, and it is not recorded as a workaround here, since it defeats the anti-cheat and puts the account at risk.

Linux (Proton) reference: 136 of 166 player reports from the last two years say it runs (ProtonDB, as of 2026-11-02, ODbL).

Mac data: Highball database (CC0).
