Half-Life on Mac

Mac players report it runs

A Mac player reported it running through Highball (5/5) (M3 Max 2026-10-04); not verified by Highball's maintainer. Not run in Soju yet; Soju's engines differ, so results can too. Their notes: The 1998 original (GoldSrc). Reported perfect after 8 minutes on an M3 Max on the default engine r17 with the environment on DXVK and the frame cap at 60. The game draws through OpenGL, which Wine hands to the Mac's own OpenGL whatever the graphics mode, so the mode is unlikely to matter for it, an inference rather than a measurement, and the mode here is the one reported. Steam also sells a native Mac build of it.…

Linux (Proton) reference: 69 of 75 player reports from the last two years say it runs (ProtonDB, as of 2026-11-02, ODbL).

How to try it with Soju

brew install BCD1210/soju/soju
soju install          # choose Steam (macOS 26 or later)
soju steam         # sign in, install the game, press Play

Or download the Soju app and add Steam under Platforms. Needs an Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or newer) and macOS 14 or later.

Tried it? Tell us in #compatibility on Discord or send a compatibility report and this page gets updated.
