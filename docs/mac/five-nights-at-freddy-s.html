Five Nights at Freddy's on Mac

Runs on Mac (Highball test)

Highball's maintainer ran it on an Apple Silicon Mac with Highball's own Wine engine and dxvk. Not run in Soju yet; Soju's engines differ, so results can too. Their notes: Plays, slower on macOS 27. On macOS 26.6.2 the menu and a night ran at a steady 60 fps on DXVK, GPU about 0.5 ms, on an Apple M1 Pro (engine x64-sikarugir10.0_6-r2). On macOS 27.0 the office, where the nights are spent, runs at about 18 to 27 fps whatever the graphics mode: 18 on DXVK (MoltenVK's own count, no screen capture running) and 25 on Wine's own Direct3D on an M4 with engine r15, 27 on DXVK with the Wine 11…

Linux (Proton) reference: 25 of 25 player reports from the last two years say it runs (ProtonDB, as of 2026-11-02, ODbL).

Mac data: Highball database (CC0).

How to try it with Soju

brew install BCD1210/soju/soju
soju install          # choose Steam (macOS 26 or later)
soju steam         # sign in, install the game, press Play

Or download the Soju app and add Steam under Platforms. Needs an Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or newer) and macOS 14 or later.

Tried it? Tell us in #compatibility on Discord or send a compatibility report and this page gets updated.
