Cities: Skylines II on Mac

Mac players report it runs

A Mac player reported it running through Highball (5/5) (M5 Max 2026-10-21); not verified by Highball's maintainer. Not run in Soju yet; Soju's engines differ, so results can too. Their notes: Reported perfect on the Wine 11 engine (x64-crossover26.3-r27) with DXMT on an M5 Max, after a short session of four minutes. No frame rate or city size was given and nothing was measured here. A second short report, on an M5 Max with the Wine 11 engine r28 (the D3DMetal 4 line) and D3DMetal by the report, rates it playable with minor issues, none named (highball-db#1050).

Linux (Proton) reference: 152 of 207 player reports from the last two years say it runs (ProtonDB, as of 2026-11-02, ODbL).

Mac data: Highball database (CC0).

How to try it with Soju

brew install BCD1210/soju/soju
soju install          # choose Steam (macOS 26 or later)
soju steam         # sign in, install the game, press Play

Or download the Soju app and add Steam under Platforms. Needs an Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or newer) and macOS 14 or later.

Tried it? Tell us in #compatibility on Discord or send a compatibility report and this page gets updated.
