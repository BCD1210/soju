Tokyo Xtreme Racer on Mac

Mac players report it runs

A Mac player reported it running through Highball (5/5) (M5 2026-10-02); not verified by Highball's maintainer. Not run in Soju yet; Soju's engines differ, so results can too. Their notes: Reported perfect on D3DMetal on the default engine once two things are set: the game's DirectX 11 build is started with -d3d11 (Steam offers 'Play Tokyo Xtreme Racer (DirectX 11)' at launch, and the reporter put -d3d11 in Steam's launch options), and the environment's graphics mode is Apple's DirectX 12 support. In-game settings all Medium with TSR anti-aliasing, which the reporter says keeps the MacBook Pro cool…

Linux (Proton) reference: 81 of 83 player reports from the last two years say it runs (ProtonDB, as of 2026-11-02, ODbL).

Mac data: Highball database (CC0).

How to try it with Soju

brew install BCD1210/soju/soju
soju install          # choose Steam (macOS 26 or later)
soju steam         # sign in, install the game, press Play

Or download the Soju app and add Steam under Platforms. Needs an Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or newer) and macOS 14 or later.

Tried it? Tell us in Discord or send a compatibility report and this page gets updated.
