Grand Theft Auto V on Mac

Online blocked by anti-cheat

GTA Online added BattlEye anti-cheat in September 2024 and since then refuses Wine and Proton, so online play does not work. Story mode has not been run in Soju yet; it installs from the Rockstar Games Launcher or Steam in Soju.

Linux (Proton) reference: 97 of 141 player reports from the last two years say it runs (ProtonDB, as of 2026-11-02, ODbL).

Highball's maintainer ran it on an Apple Silicon Mac (M1 2026-09-09) with Highball's own Wine engine and dxmt. Not run in Soju yet; Soju's engines differ, so results can too. Their notes: Story mode plays. Needs engine x64-crossover26.3-r5 or later and the DXMT renderer. Launch it from Steam: Steam starts the Rockstar Games Launcher, which signs in and hands off to the game. How to try it with Soju

brew install BCD1210/soju/soju
soju install          # choose Rockstar Games Launcher
soju rockstar      # sign in, install the game, press Play

Or download the Soju app and add Rockstar Games Launcher under Platforms. Needs an Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or newer) and macOS 14 or later.

Tried it? Tell us in #compatibility on Discord or send a compatibility report and this page gets updated.
