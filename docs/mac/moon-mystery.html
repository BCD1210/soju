Moon Mystery on Mac

Mac players report it runs

A Mac player reported it running through Highball (4/5) (M5 Pro 2026-10-18); not verified by Highball's maintainer. Not run in Soju yet; Soju's engines differ, so results can too. Their notes: Works on DXMT, the default mode, with full graphics. D3DMetal caps the resolution and forces low settings, and DXVK crashes when loading a game. On an M5 Pro with the Wine 11 engine r29 it runs at 60 fps and more at 3200x1800 fullscreen, with occasional lags while shaders load (highball#659).

Linux (Proton) reference: 6 of 7 player reports from the last two years say it runs (ProtonDB, as of 2026-11-02, ODbL).

Mac data: Highball database (CC0).

How to try it with Soju

brew install BCD1210/soju/soju
soju install          # choose Steam (macOS 26 or later)
soju steam         # sign in, install the game, press Play

Or download the Soju app and add Steam under Platforms. Needs an Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or newer) and macOS 14 or later.

Tried it? Tell us in Discord or send a compatibility report and this page gets updated.
