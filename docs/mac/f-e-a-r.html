F.E.A.R. on Mac

Does not run: anti-cheat

Its anti-cheat (PunkBuster) does not let it run under Wine or Proton, so it does not run on a Mac this way. The Linux numbers below are for Proton.

Linux (Proton) reference: 28 of 29 player reports from the last two years say it runs (ProtonDB, as of 2026-11-02, ODbL).
