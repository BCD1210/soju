Forza Horizon 5 on Mac

Mac players report problems

A Mac player reported problems through Highball (2/5) (M4 2026-10-07). Not run in Soju yet; Soju's engines differ, so results can too. Their notes: Direct3D 12, so D3DMetal. A detailed report on an M4 gets past startup, the Xbox sign-in and into free roam and the benchmark on both Wine 11 engines, but the game is not playable: every piece of scenery draws tilted and a Fatal Error ends it within minutes. Getting that far took a graphics card disguise and a hand-made way around the black sign-in window, see the known issues. Not measured by the maintainer.

Linux (Proton) reference: 250 of 311 player reports from the last two years say it runs (ProtonDB, as of 2026-11-02, ODbL).

Mac data: Highball database (CC0).

How to try it with Soju

brew install BCD1210/soju/soju
soju install          # choose Steam (macOS 26 or later)
soju steam         # sign in, install the game, press Play

Or download the Soju app and add Steam under Platforms. Needs an Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or newer) and macOS 14 or later.

Tried it? Tell us in Discord or send a compatibility report and this page gets updated.
