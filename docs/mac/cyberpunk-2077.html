Cyberpunk 2077 on Mac

Has a Mac version

CD PROJEKT RED ships a native Apple Silicon version (Mac App Store, Steam and GOG). Use that; it does not need Soju.

Linux (Proton) reference: 751 of 816 player reports from the last two years say it runs (ProtonDB, as of 2026-11-02, ODbL).

A Mac player reported it running through Highball (4/5) (M3 Max 2026-10-15); not verified by Highball's maintainer. Not run in Soju yet; Soju's engines differ, so results can too. Their notes: Playable. M4: ~17 fps avg, 1920x1200 Steam Deck preset + FSR 2.1. M5: 60-82 fps in gameplay (Metal HUD; the built-in benchmark's summary under-reads because of first-frames shader compilation), fullscreen 1920x1200, FSR 2.1 Balanced, ray-traced local shadows ON. Mandatory: Advertise AVX ON (engine crashes at launch otherwise); Steam launch option --launcher-skip (REDlauncher fails on OpenGL context / libEGL.dll);…

Mac data: Highball database (CC0).
