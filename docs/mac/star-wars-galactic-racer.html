STAR WARS: Galactic Racer™ on Mac

Mac players report it runs

A Mac player reported it running through Highball (2026-11-07); not verified by Highball's maintainer. Not run in Soju yet; Soju's engines differ, so results can too. Their notes: Doesn't start yet in one report on an M1 Mac mini: the game ends itself right after starting, before it opens a window, on the Wine 10 and Wine 11 engines. The Steam release carries Denuvo Anti-Tamper (store page).

Linux (Proton) reference: 102 of 118 player reports from the last two years say it runs (ProtonDB, as of 2026-11-02, ODbL).

Mac data: Highball database (CC0).

How to try it with Soju

brew install BCD1210/soju/soju
soju install          # choose Steam (macOS 26 or later)
soju steam         # sign in, install the game, press Play

Or download the Soju app and add Steam under Platforms. Needs an Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or newer) and macOS 14 or later.

Tried it? Tell us in #compatibility on Discord or send a compatibility report and this page gets updated.
