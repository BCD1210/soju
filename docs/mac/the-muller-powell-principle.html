THE MULLER-POWELL PRINCIPLE on Mac

Estimate: few reports

Not run on a Mac yet; this is an estimate. On Linux with Proton, 2 of 2 player reports in total say the game runs (ProtonDB, as of 2026-11-02). Soju runs Windows Steam games through Wine on macOS, with Direct3D 11 through DXMT. Direct3D 12 games are not checked in Soju's Steam setup yet, and anti-cheat or Mac-specific problems can make the result differ from Linux. If the game's Steam page lists macOS, use that version instead.

How to try it with Soju

brew install BCD1210/soju/soju
soju install          # choose Steam (macOS 26 or later)
soju steam         # sign in, install the game, press Play

Or download the Soju app and add Steam under Platforms. Needs an Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or newer) and macOS 14 or later.

Tried it? Tell us in #compatibility on Discord or send a compatibility report and this page gets updated.
