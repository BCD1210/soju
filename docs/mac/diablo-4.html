Soju is a free, open-source app that installs the official Windows game launchers on Apple Silicon Macs.

Diablo IV on Mac

Runs in Soju

Played with Blizzard's free trial on an M4 Pro with macOS 26.5: the opening cinematic played, a character was created and the game loaded into the first area, where the character moved. Direct3D 12 through Apple's D3DMetal. Battle.net asked for 97.4 GB without High-Resolution Assets (181 GB with them), and Play turned on at about 73 percent of the download. Sound, frame rate, combat and long sessions were not checked.

How to try it with Soju

brew install BCD1210/soju/soju
soju install          # choose Battle.net
soju battlenet     # sign in, install the game, press Play

Or download the Soju app and add Battle.net under Platforms. Needs an Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or newer) and macOS 14 or later.

What you need

ItemDetail
MacApple Silicon. Checked on an M4 Pro with macOS 26.5; other Macs are untested.
AccountA Battle.net account. The free trial needs no purchase; Blizzard sets its level cap and how long it is offered.
Apple's Game Porting ToolkitSoju downloads it during setup. soju doctor says if anything is missing.
Disk97.4 GB without High-Resolution Assets, 181 GB with them, as Battle.net reported it. Untick High-Resolution Assets on the install screen to save the space.

What we checked

  • Battle.net installed the trial and started the game.
  • The setup screens, the opening cinematic with subtitles, class selection and character creation all drew correctly.
  • The game loaded into the first area (Icehowl Taiga) with the interface, minimap and quest shown, and the character moved.

What we did not check

  • Sound, frame rate, combat and long sessions.
  • Playing with other people, and the full game beyond the trial.
  • Other Macs and macOS versions.

Played further on your Mac? Turn on Show FPS in the game's sheet in Soju and tell us the number in #compatibility on Discord or in a compatibility report; it goes on the Diablo IV page.

Tried it? Tell us in #compatibility on Discord or send a compatibility report and this page gets updated.
