Command & Conquer 3: Tiberium Wars on Mac

Mac players report it runs

A Mac player reported it running through Highball (5/5) (M2 2026-10-04); not verified by Highball's maintainer. Not run in Soju yet; Soju's engines differ, so results can too. Their notes: A 2007 Direct3D 9 real-time strategy game. Reported perfect after 25 minutes on an M2 with the environment on DXMT on the Wine 11 engine r12. DXMT itself handles Direct3D 10 and 11 only, and on the Wine 11 engine Highball's automatic modes leave Direct3D 9 to Wine's own Direct3D (the rule the Fallout: New Vegas report confirmed in its log), so that is most likely what drew the game, an inference from the app's rule…

Linux (Proton) reference: 15 of 16 player reports from the last two years say it runs (ProtonDB, as of 2026-11-02, ODbL).

Mac data: Highball database (CC0).

How to try it with Soju

brew install BCD1210/soju/soju
soju install          # choose Steam (macOS 26 or later)
soju steam         # sign in, install the game, press Play

Or download the Soju app and add Steam under Platforms. Needs an Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or newer) and macOS 14 or later.

Tried it? Tell us in Discord or send a compatibility report and this page gets updated.
