Yu-Gi-Oh! Master Duel on Mac

Mac players report it runs

A Mac player reported it running through Highball (5/5) (M4 Pro 2026-09-22); not verified by Highball's maintainer. Not run in Soju yet; Soju's engines differ, so results can too. Their notes: Reported perfect four times on DXMT on the default engine, most recently on an M5 with r29 and macOS 27.2 (49 minutes, Retina pixels, 60 fps cap). On an M4 Pro with macOS 26.6.2 and engine r5 the Metal HUD hovered around 110 to 120 fps over a 39-minute session, by the reporter's follow-up. On an M4 with macOS 15.7.9, Highball 0.10.3 and engine r19 a 221-minute session was perfect apart from fullscreen being covered…

Linux (Proton) reference: 26 of 28 player reports from the last two years say it runs (ProtonDB, as of 2026-11-02, ODbL).

Mac data: Highball database (CC0).

How to try it with Soju

brew install BCD1210/soju/soju
soju install          # choose Steam (macOS 26 or later)
soju steam         # sign in, install the game, press Play

Or download the Soju app and add Steam under Platforms. Needs an Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or newer) and macOS 14 or later.

Tried it? Tell us in Discord or send a compatibility report and this page gets updated.
