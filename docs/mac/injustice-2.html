Injustice 2 on Mac

Mac players report it runs

A Mac player reported it running through Highball (4/5) (M1 2026-11-04); not verified by Highball's maintainer. Not run in Soju yet; Soju's engines differ, so results can too. Their notes: Reported playable on an M1 with D3DMetal over 11 minutes, smooth at about 45 fps in fights. The cutscenes jitter at 30 fps on the default Wine 10 engine, and on a Wine 11 engine their colors are wrong too. The Metal HUD in the reporter's screenshots counts only two draws a frame in a cutscene on Wine 10 and nine on Wine 11, so they are most likely videos rather than scenes drawn in real time (inferred, not checked).

Linux (Proton) reference: 10 of 16 player reports from the last two years say it runs (ProtonDB, as of 2026-11-02, ODbL).

Mac data: Highball database (CC0).

How to try it with Soju

brew install BCD1210/soju/soju
soju install          # choose Steam (macOS 26 or later)
soju steam         # sign in, install the game, press Play

Or download the Soju app and add Steam under Platforms. Needs an Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or newer) and macOS 14 or later.

Tried it? Tell us in Discord or send a compatibility report and this page gets updated.
