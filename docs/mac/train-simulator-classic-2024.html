Train Simulator Classic 2024 on Mac

Mac players report it runs

A Mac player reported it running through Highball (5/5) (2026-09-15); not verified by Highball's maintainer. Not run in Soju yet; Soju's engines differ, so results can too. Their notes: Reported to start and run perfectly on the low preset over a two-hour session on DXMT. On the fanless MacBook Neo the frame rate settled around 30 to 40 fps after a while as the machine warmed up, still playable. A Mac with active cooling should hold higher settings. No measurement here yet.

Linux (Proton) reference: 10 of 11 player reports from the last two years say it runs (ProtonDB, as of 2026-11-02, ODbL).

Mac data: Highball database (CC0).

How to try it with Soju

brew install BCD1210/soju/soju
soju install          # choose Steam (macOS 26 or later)
soju steam         # sign in, install the game, press Play

Or download the Soju app and add Steam under Platforms. Needs an Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or newer) and macOS 14 or later.

Tried it? Tell us in #compatibility on Discord or send a compatibility report and this page gets updated.
