Marvel's Guardians of the Galaxy on Mac

Runs on Mac (Highball test)

Highball's maintainer ran it on an Apple Silicon Mac (M5 2026-09-09) with Highball's own Wine engine and d3dmetal. Not run in Soju yet; Soju's engines differ, so results can too. Their notes: Playable on the r5 engine, which carries the fix. DirectX 12 only: on DXMT it stops at "No suitable D3D12 adapter found" before the splash, so D3DMetal is the only mode that reaches the game, and Highball asks for Apple's licence at the first launch. Reached gameplay twice from a clean start on 2026-09-09: 27.5 fps at 1280x720 in the opening scene, 17-32 fps in play, and about 10 fps for the first minute while…

Linux (Proton) reference: 30 of 33 player reports from the last two years say it runs (ProtonDB, as of 2026-11-02, ODbL).

Mac data: Highball database (CC0).

How to try it with Soju

brew install BCD1210/soju/soju
soju install          # choose Steam (macOS 26 or later)
soju steam         # sign in, install the game, press Play

Or download the Soju app and add Steam under Platforms. Needs an Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or newer) and macOS 14 or later.

Tried it? Tell us in #compatibility on Discord or send a compatibility report and this page gets updated.
