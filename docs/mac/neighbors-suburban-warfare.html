Neighbors: Suburban Warfare on Mac

Mac players report it runs

A Mac player reported it running through Highball (5/5) (M4 Max 2026-10-04); not verified by Highball's maintainer. Not run in Soju yet; Soju's engines differ, so results can too. Their notes: An online player-versus-player game, Direct3D 11 by its store page. Reported perfect on DXMT on the default engine r17 on an M4 Max after a 13-minute session. The anti-cheat map has no entry for it, and Steam's public launch entry starts the game directly, a protected launch through start_protected_game.exe (Easy Anti-Cheat's launcher) exists only on the developer branches of its Steam configuration, read from that…

Linux (Proton) reference: 6 of 7 player reports from the last two years say it runs (ProtonDB, as of 2026-11-02, ODbL).

Mac data: Highball database (CC0).

How to try it with Soju

brew install BCD1210/soju/soju
soju install          # choose Steam (macOS 26 or later)
soju steam         # sign in, install the game, press Play

Or download the Soju app and add Steam under Platforms. Needs an Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or newer) and macOS 14 or later.

Tried it? Tell us in #compatibility on Discord or send a compatibility report and this page gets updated.
