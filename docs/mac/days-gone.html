Days Gone on Mac

Mac players report it runs

A Mac player reported it running through Highball (3/5) (M5 Pro 2026-11-10); not verified by Highball's maintainer. Not run in Soju yet; Soju's engines differ, so results can too. Their notes: Plays on an M5 Pro with DXMT on the Wine 11 engine r30 at 60 fps once the four intro videos are moved out of BendGame/Content/Movies (highball-db#1139). Steam's Verify files and game updates put them back. Untested here: disabling Media Foundation (mfplat) for DaysGone.exe made other Unreal games skip their videos (Fort Solis), and a DLL override is something a recipe can set. How to try it with Soju

brew install BCD1210/soju/soju
soju install          # choose Steam (macOS 26 or later)
soju steam         # sign in, install the game, press Play

Or download the Soju app and add Steam under Platforms. Needs an Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or newer) and macOS 14 or later.

Tried it? Tell us in Discord or send a compatibility report and this page gets updated.
