Counter-Strike 2 on Mac

Not checked in Soju yet

Valve dropped the Mac version with Counter-Strike 2. It has not been run in Soju yet. It installs from the Windows Steam client in Soju (macOS 26 or later).

Linux (Proton) reference: 732 of 868 player reports from the last two years say it runs (ProtonDB, as of 2026-11-02, ODbL).

Highball's maintainer ran it on an Apple Silicon Mac (M5 2026-09-17) with Highball's own Wine engine and dxmt. Not run in Soju yet; Soju's engines differ, so results can too. Their notes: Use DXMT on macOS 15 or newer: a bot match on Dust II ran at 102-108 fps at 1728x1117 fullscreen with the game's defaults, 10.3 ms of GPU per frame and a 9.3 ms frame interval by the Metal HUD, engine r2, DXMT 0.80 (2026-09-10, macOS 26.6.2, M1 Pro). How to try it with Soju

brew install BCD1210/soju/soju
soju install          # choose Steam (macOS 26 or later)
soju steam         # sign in, install the game, press Play

Or download the Soju app and add Steam under Platforms. Needs an Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or newer) and macOS 14 or later.

Tried it? Tell us in #compatibility on Discord or send a compatibility report and this page gets updated.
