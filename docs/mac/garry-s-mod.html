Garry's Mod on Mac

Mac players report it runs

A Mac player reported it running through Highball (4/5) (M5 2026-09-18); not verified by Highball's maintainer. Not run in Soju yet; Soju's engines differ, so results can too. Their notes: Source engine, Direct3D 9. Boots and plays fine at the display's native resolution in the one report, completely playable over a near-hour session. Two things noted: it crashed once while creating a world for the first time and was fine after a restart, and motion feels a little jittery, not a true 120 Hz feel, even though the game itself runs well. Frame rate not measured.

Linux (Proton) reference: 187 of 221 player reports from the last two years say it runs (ProtonDB, as of 2026-11-02, ODbL).

Mac data: Highball database (CC0).

How to try it with Soju

brew install BCD1210/soju/soju
soju install          # choose Steam (macOS 26 or later)
soju steam         # sign in, install the game, press Play

Or download the Soju app and add Steam under Platforms. Needs an Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or newer) and macOS 14 or later.

Tried it? Tell us in #compatibility on Discord or send a compatibility report and this page gets updated.
