Fallout: New Vegas on Mac

Mac players report problems

A Mac player reported problems through Highball (2/5) (M3 Pro 2026-09-25). Not run in Soju yet; Soju's engines differ, so results can too. Their notes: A Direct3D 9 game from 2010. On the Wine 11 engines DXVK's Direct3D 9 runs at about half the speed the default engine's does (Five Nights at Freddy's, 40 against 76 fps, measured 2026-09-25). Wine's own Direct3D on r11 helped the reporter but did not make it playable, so the CPU side is the next suspect. Engine r12 carries the loader side of x87 acceleration only. The sidecar binary that does the work is not shipped…

How to try it with Soju

brew install BCD1210/soju/soju
soju install          # choose Steam (macOS 26 or later)
soju steam         # sign in, install the game, press Play

Or download the Soju app and add Steam under Platforms. Needs an Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or newer) and macOS 14 or later.

Tried it? Tell us in Discord or send a compatibility report and this page gets updated.
