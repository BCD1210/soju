Age of Empires II: Definitive Edition on Mac

Mac players report problems

A Mac player reported problems through Highball (1/5) (M3 Pro 2026-10-08). Not run in Soju yet; Soju's engines differ, so results can too. Their notes: This game has a native macOS version on Steam, so the Mac Steam app runs it directly without Highball. Through Highball, the Windows build crashed before its splash screen on the Wine 10 engine (r21) and ran on the Wine 11 engine (r19, DXMT) for the same player. No measurement here yet.

Linux (Proton) reference: 132 of 150 player reports from the last two years say it runs (ProtonDB, as of 2026-11-02, ODbL).

Mac data: Highball database (CC0).

How to try it with Soju

brew install BCD1210/soju/soju
soju install          # choose Steam (macOS 26 or later)
soju steam         # sign in, install the game, press Play

Or download the Soju app and add Steam under Platforms. Needs an Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or newer) and macOS 14 or later.

Tried it? Tell us in Discord or send a compatibility report and this page gets updated.
