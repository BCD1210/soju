Age of Mythology: Retold on Mac

Mac players report it runs

A Mac player reported it running through Highball (5/5) (M4 Pro 2026-10-03); not verified by Highball's maintainer. Not run in Soju yet; Soju's engines differ, so results can too. Their notes: Reported perfect on D3DMetal on the Wine 11 engine r15 on an M4 Pro. Two of the reporter's settings match what the game's own Steam page asks for, DirectX 12 and a processor with AVX. D3DMetal is the DirectX 12 route, and AVX advertised lets the game see AVX. How to try it with Soju

brew install BCD1210/soju/soju
soju install          # choose Steam (macOS 26 or later)
soju steam         # sign in, install the game, press Play

Or download the Soju app and add Steam under Platforms. Needs an Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or newer) and macOS 14 or later.

Tried it? Tell us in #compatibility on Discord or send a compatibility report and this page gets updated.
