Marvel’s Spider-Man: Miles Morales on Mac

Mac players report it runs

A Mac player reported it running through Highball (5/5) (M4 2026-10-15); not verified by Highball's maintainer. Not run in Soju yet; Soju's engines differ, so results can too. Their notes: Reported perfect on D3DMetal on the Wine 11 engine r25, about 46 fps on Medium in the intro on a base M4 with 16 GB, from an external SSD. The reporter also had the DLL overrides d3d12,d3d12core=n,b set and ran the game under Windows 11; whether either matters was not tested.

Linux (Proton) reference: 30 of 30 player reports from the last two years say it runs (ProtonDB, as of 2026-11-02, ODbL).

Mac data: Highball database (CC0).

How to try it with Soju

brew install BCD1210/soju/soju
soju install          # choose Steam (macOS 26 or later)
soju steam         # sign in, install the game, press Play

Or download the Soju app and add Steam under Platforms. Needs an Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or newer) and macOS 14 or later.

Tried it? Tell us in #compatibility on Discord or send a compatibility report and this page gets updated.
