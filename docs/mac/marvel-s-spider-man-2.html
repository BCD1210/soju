Marvel's Spider-Man 2 on Mac

Mac players report it runs

A Mac player reported it running through Highball (4/5) (M4 Pro 2026-11-01); not verified by Highball's maintainer. Not run in Soju yet; Soju's engines differ, so results can too. Their notes: Reported playable on an M4 Pro with D3DMetal over two hours, with AVX advertised, D3DM_MTL4=1 and D3DM_SUPPORT_DXR=1 set by the player. A player on an M4 Pro rates it playable with the sound glitching at times, and uses MacGameFix for characters stuck in a T-pose (highball-db#1133).

Linux (Proton) reference: 93 of 112 player reports from the last two years say it runs (ProtonDB, as of 2026-11-02, ODbL).

Mac data: Highball database (CC0).

How to try it with Soju

brew install BCD1210/soju/soju
soju install          # choose Steam (macOS 26 or later)
soju steam         # sign in, install the game, press Play

Or download the Soju app and add Steam under Platforms. Needs an Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or newer) and macOS 14 or later.

Tried it? Tell us in Discord or send a compatibility report and this page gets updated.
