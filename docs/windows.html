Don't Sell the Cat on Windows

You bought the game from us and want to play on a PC. Here's how.

Install

  1. Open the Polar receipt email (or the page you saw after paying) and download DontSellTheCat-windows-1.0.zip. It's about 375 MB.
  2. Right-click the zip, choose Extract All, and put the folder wherever you like.
  3. Open the folder and double-click DontSellTheCat.exe.
  4. If a blue window says "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway. It shows up because the game isn't from the Microsoft Store and we don't pay for a code-signing certificate. You only see it the first time.

Needs Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) and a graphics card with DirectX 12. On older laptops, set Graphics to Low in Settings.

Saves are in %APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\Don't Sell the Cat. Copy that folder to move your progress to another PC.

Updates

New versions are added to your purchase. Download the zip again from the receipt link and replace the old folder; your saves stay where they are.

On a Mac, use Soju instead: open the Didim Labs Store tab and paste your license key.
