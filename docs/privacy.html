Privacy

Soju is a Mac app made by Didim Labs. It keeps your game library on your Mac. This page lists everything that leaves it.

Signing in with Apple or Google

  • We ask Apple or Google only for your email address. We don't ask for contacts, files or anything else.
  • Soju asks you to sign in before buying a game or getting a demo in the Didim Labs Store. We use that email to find games you bought from us through Polar, and hand the license keys back to your copy of Soju.
  • Our server keeps the sign-in result for at most 10 minutes, then deletes it. Soju keeps your email and keys on your Mac (keys in the macOS Keychain).
  • Your Didim Labs account is that email, the provider (Apple or Google) and the days you first and last signed in. We keep it so your games and demos stay with you; a purchase made from Soju uses the same email. Write to us to delete it.
  • If you use Apple's Hide My Email, we only see the relay address, so purchases made with your real email won't be found.

Buying games

Payments go through Polar, which acts as the seller of record and handles card details, receipts and taxes. We see your email, name, country and what you bought. We never see card numbers.

What the Soju app sends

  • Soju downloads the store list and the sponsor card from didimlabs.com, and game files from Polar or GitHub.
  • When it opens, Soju asks GitHub for the list of Soju releases to tell you if a newer version is out. The request says nothing about you or your games.
  • Account connections you add (Steam, GOG and others) talk to those services directly from your Mac.
  • If you turn on TV & iPad streaming, Soju installs Sunshine (open source, LizardByte) on your Mac. It streams only to devices you pair with a PIN on your own network, keeps its settings page on your Mac, and only contacts GitHub to check for new versions.
  • Play counts (on unless you turn them off): Soju sends which games are installed (once a day) and how many minutes a game stayed open, with your Mac model, chip, macOS and Soju version and a random id made on your Mac. When a game closes within a minute or does not start, it also sends a short reason read on your Mac from Soju's launch log: one word (such as crash or missing file), the name of the missing file or the error number, and which anti-cheat the game uses. The log itself stays on your Mac. No account, email, name or file path. The server keeps the internet address a report came from for a short time (normally a few hours), only to limit abuse, and then deletes it. We use the counts to see which games run. The game pages on this site may show a game as played in Soju from the totals for that game; nothing about one person or one Mac is published. Turn it off under Stores (Share which games I play) or with soju stats off.
  • Soju has no ads tracking or crash reporting.

The Didim Labs feed

  • The feed in Soju's Didim Labs page is for people signed in with Apple or Google. Reading it sends a sign-in token that Soju keeps on your Mac.
  • If you post, we keep the nickname you pick, your posts and replies, the photos you attach, your likes and your reports of other posts, tied to your account email. Other people see the nickname, never the email.
  • Deleting a post in Soju deletes it, its photos and its replies from our server. To delete the account and everything posted under it, write to hello@didimlabs.com.

This website

didimlabs.com runs on Cloudflare, which keeps standard request logs. Pages load Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts page views and load times without cookies and without identifying you. The site sets no cookies. If you send the advertising form, we get what you type and reply by email.

Release news emails

If you type your email to hear about our next game, we keep that address only for that. We send an email when a new game comes out and nothing else. Write to hello@didimlabs.com to be removed.

Questions or deletion

Email hello@didimlabs.com. We can tell you what we hold and delete it, except records Polar must keep for tax law. To remove everything from your Mac, sign out in Soju and delete the app.
