Privacy

Soju is a Mac app made by Didim Labs. It keeps your game library on your Mac. This page lists everything that leaves it.

Signing in with Apple or Google

  • We ask Apple or Google only for your email address. We don't ask for contacts, files or anything else.
  • We use that email once, to find games you bought from us through Polar, and hand the license keys back to your copy of Soju.
  • Our server keeps the result for at most 10 minutes, then deletes it. Soju keeps your email and keys on your Mac (keys in the macOS Keychain).
  • If you use Apple's Hide My Email, we only see the relay address, so purchases made with your real email won't be found.

Buying games

Payments go through Polar, which acts as the seller of record and handles card details, receipts and taxes. We see your email, name, country and what you bought. We never see card numbers.

What the Soju app sends

  • Soju downloads the store list and the sponsor card from didimlabs.com, and game files from Polar or GitHub.
  • When it opens, Soju asks GitHub for the list of Soju releases to tell you if a newer version is out. The request says nothing about you or your games.
  • Account connections you add (Steam, GOG and others) talk to those services directly from your Mac.
  • If you turn on TV & iPad streaming, Soju installs Sunshine (open source, LizardByte) on your Mac. It streams only to devices you pair with a PIN on your own network, keeps its settings page on your Mac, and only contacts GitHub to check for new versions.
  • Soju has no analytics, ads tracking or crash reporting.

This website

didimlabs.com runs on Cloudflare, which keeps standard request logs. The site sets no cookies. If you send the advertising form, we get what you type and reply by email.

Release news emails

If you type your email to hear about our next game, we keep that address only for that. We send an email when a new game comes out and nothing else. Write to hello@didimlabs.com to be removed.

Questions or deletion

Email hello@didimlabs.com. We can tell you what we hold and delete it, except records Polar must keep for tax law. To remove everything from your Mac, sign out in Soju and delete the app.
