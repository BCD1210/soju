https://soju.snack-wrap.com/support.html. This is a static page with no payment
embed, analytics or donation pop-up.

## Activate donations

1. Complete the chosen provider's recipient and payout setup.
2. Verify that the public profile belongs to the maintainer/Soju and can accept
   contributions. Never use a guessed username or another project's link.
3. Replace that card's Coming soon status with the verified HTTPS profile URL.
   Label the one-time action Buy a coffee and the recurring action Sponsor on GitHub.
4. Add only the verified account to .github/FUNDING.yml once active.
5. Check the public support page and follow each link before publishing.

Keep both financial and non-financial support voluntary. Do not add feature gates,
interruptions or promises of priority support. Keep the commission disclosure next
to purchase link information and preserve the in-app affiliate badge/disclosure.

See purchase-affiliates.md for approved product link activation.
