Tom Clancy’s The Division 2 맥에서 하기

안 됨: 안티치트

Highball 데이터베이스에서 안티치트 등으로 막힌 게임으로 분류합니다. 소주에서는 아직 실행하지 않았고 엔진이 달라 결과가 다를 수 있습니다. 메모(영문): Not playable on a Mac. The game uses Easy Anti-Cheat, which works on Linux through its runtime for Proton, and that runtime has no macOS build. The reporter confirms the anti-cheat check crashes the game window. They could only keep playing by repeatedly stopping the check from running and do not recommend it, and it is not recorded as a workaround here, since it defeats the anti-cheat and puts the account at risk.
