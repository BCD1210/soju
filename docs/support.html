Support through a game purchase.

When a store link in Soju is marked “Supports Soju”, we may earn a commission if you buy through it, at no extra cost to you. Your purchase is completed with the store.

These links appear when an affiliate option is available. Other store links work normally and do not earn Soju a commission. Affiliate availability does not affect search ranking or displayed prices.

A store listing is not a guarantee that a game will run on your Mac. Check the compatibility notes and the store's requirements before buying.
