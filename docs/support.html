Supporting Soju

Soju is free and will stay free. You don't need to pay for anything to use it.

If you want to pay something

Buy one of our games. Don't Sell the Cat is $6.99 for Mac and Windows, and that money is what pays for work on Soju.

We don't take donations yet.

"Supports Soju" store links

Some store links in Soju's Discover page may later be marked Supports Soju. Buying through one of those can earn us a commission from the store; the price is the same for you. Links without the mark earn nothing, and the mark never changes search order or prices. None are switched on yet.

A game being for sale doesn't mean it runs on a Mac. Check what we've tested first.

Helping without money

  • Report a game you tried, whether it worked or not, with your Mac and macOS version.
  • Send fixes, translations or clearer docs through GitHub.
  • Tell a friend who games on a Mac.
